Case Study

Patent Application Details · 202541055330 A

INDIAN PATENT APPLICATION

Gesture Controlled Mouse Interface for Physically Impaired Users

Role: Co-inventor | Published & Pending (App No. 202541055330 A)

01 / Technical Architecture

MediaPipe 21 hand-landmark mesh coordinates [X, Y, Z] are extracted from standard RGB camera video streams. Spatial vectors between index fingertip, thumb, and wrist form a real-time finite state machine mapping gestures to operating system events (pointer movement, left/right click, drag, scroll).

02 / Assistive Impact

Designed specifically for individuals afflicted with motor impairment, cerebral palsy, or severe arthritis who cannot grip physical hardware mice. Zero calibration delay, no wearable sensor requirement, 60fps low-latency OS integration.

03 / Interactive 21-Landmark Gesture Simulator
STATE: POINTER_MOVE (Tracking Index Tip Spatial [X, Y, Z])
